About RON

Meet Rosemond Owusu Nyarko, RSW

Rosemond is a kind, compassionate and committed Registered Social Worker who brings over 20 years of experience in the human services field.

She has worked with children, adolescents, youth, adults with disabilities and families in distress. Authenticity, spirituality, a faith-based foundation, respect and a genuinely open-minded nature are at the core of her work with every client.

Rosemond understands that in life we all face challenges, struggles, ups and downs, and that these can lead to difficult thoughts and emotions about our experiences and about ourselves. She will work collaboratively with you to make sure your needs are truly being met.

Understanding mental health

Mental health is how we think, feel and act.

Mental health is our emotional, psychological and social well-being. It includes our emotions, our feelings of connection and our thoughts, and it affects how we think, feel and act.

Throughout our lives, each of us will have times of positive and negative mental health, and this shapes everyday life: how we handle stress, how we engage with others and the choices we make.

Cognitive Behavioural Therapy (CBT)

CBT teaches you how your thoughts, feelings and behaviours work together. A goal of CBT is to identify and change the unhelpful patterns of thinking that feed negative stress reactions, depression and anxiety.

Rosemond’s role is to give you the coping skills, right-fit strategies and psychoeducation to reach a balanced way of thinking that supports both mental and physical health.

Policies & questions

Good to know before you book

Clear expectations help sessions run smoothly. If anything is unclear, just ask during your free consultation.

What is the cancellation policy?

Appointments cancelled or missed with less than 24 hours’ notice will be billed the full fee on the same day as the cancelled or missed appointment. Some exceptions apply.

Will my insurance cover missed sessions?

Missed or cancelled appointments may not be covered under your insurance plan. You are expected to keep track of your own appointments.

Can you provide documentation for insurance or legal matters?

Rosemond does not provide documentation directly for insurance or legal matters. Please refer to your primary care physician or family doctor.

Is the first consultation really free?

Yes. Every individual is unique, so the first conversation is a free consultation to get to know you and talk about what you’re looking for.

Is faith part of counselling?

Spirituality and faith are part of Rosemond’s foundation, and she welcomes them into sessions when that’s meaningful to you. Your values lead; you are always respected wherever you are.
