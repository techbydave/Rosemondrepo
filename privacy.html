Privacy

Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters. This page explains what happens to information on this website.

Information collected by this website

This website does not use accounts, cookies for tracking, or analytics, and it does not store any information you type. The consultation request form does not send data to a server: when you press “Send request”, it opens your own email app with a pre-written message addressed to Ronfamily.agape@outlook.com. Nothing is sent until you choose to send that email yourself.

Emails and phone calls

If you contact us by email or phone, the information you share is used only to respond to you and arrange services. Email is not a fully secure channel, so please keep sensitive personal or health details for your session rather than your first message.

Confidentiality in counselling

What you share in counselling is kept confidential, within the limits set by law and professional standards for Registered Social Workers in Ontario. Rosemond will explain these limits, and how your records are kept, at the start of working together.

Third-party services

This site loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address as part of delivering the font files.

Questions

For any privacy questions, email Ronfamily.agape@outlook.com or call +1 (416) 779-9784.
